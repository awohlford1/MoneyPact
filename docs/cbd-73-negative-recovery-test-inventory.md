## 1. Purpose and assertion contract

This inventory supplies the negative and recovery acceptance evidence for the CBD-73 lifecycle. Every completed scenario states the actor and membership or possession state, budget space, invitation/membership/proposal state and versions at start, action, authoritative expected result, protected-state delta, audit outcome, notification outcome, idempotency key where applicable, and the customer-visible recovery.

A denied scenario passes only when the action is unavailable where it should be, direct requests deny server-side, protected state is unchanged, no restricted data is returned or inferred, and the denial records exactly the approved audit projection. An atomic scenario passes only when every authoritative record, code/proof disposition, consent record, membership/scope change, destination association, outbox entry, and audit event commits together or none does. Retried work must converge on the first authoritative outcome without duplicate effects.

Scenario IDs are globally unique and never reused or renumbered. Every CBD-73 scenario uses the `FAMILY-73-NN` form; the family prefix alone is never a stable identifier.

## 3. Open issues controlling expected outcomes

> [!WARNING]
> **OPEN ISSUE `OI-73-001` — wrong-recipient and misdirected-invitation policy.** `VER-73-02` and `VER-73-08` define the privacy ceiling and correction evidence, but their final accept/deny oracle and any remedy beyond ordinary role-appropriate removal require a Product Owner/source decision.

> [!WARNING]
> **OPEN ISSUE `OI-73-002` — alternative no-account decline UX.** `VER-73-07` and `DCL-73-13` enforce the settled interim rule: after exact invited-channel verification, a holder without an authenticated account may decline this invitation once; persistent decline-and-block is unavailable until an authenticated account is attached; and ambiguity, abandonment, or interruption creates neither. Protected disclosure, destination nomination, consent, and acceptance remain authenticated-account-only. The Product Owner/source decision is open only on whether a different no-account UX should replace this interim behavior.

> [!WARNING]
> **OPEN ISSUE `OI-73-003` — CBD-91 data classes and focused source alignment.** Final class, audience, retention, and deletion mappings for synthetic suppression, ceremony/account/destination proofs, outcome reconciliation, membership-scoped destinations, and subject lifecycle notices remain open, as does the focused CBD-72/CBD-91 source alignment required by `RI-93-012` to replace older every-channel lifecycle routing. This does not reopen CBD-73's normative rule: lifecycle notice is mandatory in-app, may additionally use only a verified private safety channel, has no ordinary-channel fallback, and remains in-app-only when that safety channel is unavailable. Fixtures may prove least disclosure and no-fallback behavior but cannot approve the class mappings or external source edits.

> [!WARNING]
> **OPEN ISSUE `OI-73-004` — exact copy, decline/expiry presentation, accessibility, and localization.** Scenarios assert semantic equivalence and prohibited disclosure only. Exact strings, safe non-attributing decline/expiry presentation, locale equivalence, accessibility/comprehension, and specialist review remain open.

> [!WARNING]
> **OPEN ISSUE `OI-73-008` — concrete code, verification, and rate design.** Entropy, proof mechanism, expiry duration, bounded-attempt values, recipient-key derivation, and resource/rate parameters remain architecture evidence, not approved values in these scenarios.

> [!WARNING]
> **OPEN ISSUE `OI-73-009` — invalidation mechanism evidence.** Session, cache, job, queue, package, destination, and in-flight-request invalidation outcomes are normative; exact mechanisms and measured cutoff evidence remain open.

> [!WARNING]
> **OPEN ISSUE `OI-73-010` — inviter-block and pair-limiter engineering.** Account-bound block matching, alias/token handling, privacy-preserving recipient and inviter key derivation, separation of no-account limiter evaluation from account-only block evaluation, synthetic-record storage, limiter values, recovery, response padding, and abuse evidence remain open even though non-enumerating outcomes are normative.

> [!WARNING]
> **OPEN ISSUE `OI-73-011` — audit storage.** Event cardinality, audience projection, redaction, and required correlations are testable here; integrity, retention, storage, and operational access design remain open.

> [!WARNING]
> **OPEN ISSUE `OI-73-012` — account creation and sign-in mechanics.** The canonical order and authority boundaries are normative, while identity-provider, new-account, session-assurance, and return-to-ceremony mechanics remain open.
