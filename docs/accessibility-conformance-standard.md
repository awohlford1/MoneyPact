## 2. Conformance standard adopted

MoneyPact adopts **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** (W3C Web Content Accessibility
Guidelines, version 2.2, Level AA success criteria) as its accessibility
conformance target for:

- every authenticated surface of the product (budget spaces, schedules,
  identity and account-management pages, and every other page reachable only
  after sign-in); and
- every public surface of the product (marketing, sign-in, sign-up, and any
  other page reachable without authentication).

This target applies uniformly. No surface is exempt from the standard itself;
§3 records the specific, individually justified pairings within the token
system that do not meet a numeric contrast minimum, and why each one is
still consistent with WCAG 2.2 AA rather than a departure from it.

A provider-rendered surface (for example a managed identity provider's own
templates) is bound by this standard to the extent MoneyPact controls it.
Where MoneyPact does not control the rendered DOM, `OI-102-007`'s underlying
concern stands: this document fixes MoneyPact's own conformance target, not a
vendor's, and a vendor surface's conformance is evaluated separately with its
own evidence (CBD-102 §4.1, CBD-104 §2.1).

## 3. Known measured exceptions

[`apps/web/src/styles/contrast-record.md`](../apps/web/src/styles/contrast-record.md)
is generated by `node scripts/check-tokens.mjs --write-record` from the
`light-dark()` values in `tokens.css` and the pairings declared in
[`apps/web/src/styles/contrast-pairings.json`](../apps/web/src/styles/contrast-pairings.json).
Every pairing in that record is either measured against the WCAG 2.2 AA
minimum (4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for non-text boundaries and indicators) and
passes, or is marked `exempt` — measured and recorded, not enforced, under a
named clause. The exempt pairings fall into exactly two exception classes, in
line with the two the September 18, 2026 decision refers to. Both classes,
and their rationale, are reproduced here verbatim from the contrast record's
"Use / exemption" column.

### 3.1 Exception 1 — WCAG 1.4.3, text of inactive components

| Foreground | Background | Light | Dark | Rationale (verbatim from `contrast-record.md`) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `on-surface-disabled` | `surface` | 2.69:1 | 3.59:1 | "Disabled labels and disabled link text. WCAG 1.4.3 exempts text of inactive components." |
| `on-surface-disabled` | `surface-raised` | 2.99:1 | 3.23:1 | "Disabled control text inside a raised surface. WCAG 1.4.3 exempts text of inactive components." |

WCAG 1.4.3 (Contrast (Minimum)) does not apply to text of inactive user
interface components. These two pairings measure below 4.5:1 in both themes
and are not required to meet it; the exemption is the success criterion's own
text, not a product-specific departure from it.

### 3.2 Exception 2 — WCAG 1.4.11, non-text contrast for boundaries not required to identify a component

| Foreground | Background | Light | Dark | Rationale (verbatim from `contrast-record.md`) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `focus` | `accent` | 6.12:1 | 1.25:1 | "Focus ring around a primary button. The ring is offset 2px onto the surface and never touches the fill; the governing pairings are focus on surface and on surface-raised." |
| `accent` | `surface` | 1.12:1 | 14.03:1 | "Primary button fill on the page ground. WCAG 1.4.11: the fill's boundary is not required to identify the control; its on-accent label (a text pairing above) does. No control may rely on this fill alone." |
| `accent` | `surface-raised` | 1.25:1 | 12.65:1 | "Primary button fill inside a card or dialog. WCAG 1.4.11, as above." |
| `border` | `surface` | 1.22:1 | 1.45:1 | "Card edges, table row lines, section frames. Decorative separation between regions; WCAG 1.4.11 applies to boundaries needed to identify a component." |
| `border` | `surface-raised` | 1.36:1 | 1.30:1 | "Row lines inside a table on a raised surface. Decorative, as above." |

WCAG 1.4.11 (Non-text Contrast) requires a 3:1 minimum only for a visual
boundary that is itself required to identify a component or its state or
boundary. Each of these five pairings measures below 3:1 in at least one
theme, and each is exempt because a different, passing pairing carries the
identification requirement instead: the primary-button label text (§3, `on-accent`
on `accent`, 10.71:1/14.03:1) identifies the button, not its fill or the focus
ring's overlap with the fill; and card/table borders are decorative
separators, not component-identifying boundaries.

### 3.3 Scope of these exceptions

These two exception classes are the only pairings in the token system that do
not meet a numeric WCAG 2.2 AA minimum. Every other pairing in
`contrast-pairings.json` passes its minimum in both themes, as recorded in
`contrast-record.md`. If a future pairing is added and needs a third
exception class, or if either of these two pairings' governing rationale
changes, `contrast-record.md` and this section must be updated together —
this document does not itself gate that check; `scripts/check-tokens.mjs`
does.

## 4. Evidence versus conformance-claim discipline

Adopting a conformance target here does not certify that the product
currently conforms to it. Certifying full WCAG 2.2 AA conformance requires an
audit broader than any single page's own tests — coverage across every
surface, every assistive-technology combination, and a manual review this
document does not itself perform.

Accordingly:

- A page's, packet's, or feature's own automated and manual tests **may**
  assert only what they actually measured: for example, "tested against WCAG
  2.2 AA with zero axe-core violations (tags `wcag2a`, `wcag2aa`, `wcag21aa`)
  at the stated viewports," optionally with the specific keyboard or
  screen-reader walkthrough performed.
- No page's, packet's, or feature's own tests **may assert or imply** that
  the product, or that surface, has been certified as conforming to WCAG 2.2
  AA. A conformance claim beyond "tested against, zero violations found at
  this scope" is not supported by a single page's evidence and is exactly the
  kind of unsupported claim `EXC-76-004` forbids.
- This discipline matches `UI-BUILD-PLAN.md` §6.1 item 0, which this document
  now gives an approved home: "Conformance is tested against, never claimed."
  Every UI packet's `accessibility()` harness call (axe over `main` with
  `wcag2a, wcag2aa, wcag21aa`, asserting zero violations at the default
  viewport, at 320 CSS pixels, and at 320×225 at 4x device scale factor) is
  evidence of testing against this standard, not a certification of it.

## 6. Findings, assumptions, and dependencies

1. **Assumption — document naming.** The packet suggested a `docs/cbd-XXX-*`
   name if a free CBD number could be confirmed against both the repository
   and Jira without collision. The highest existing `docs/cbd-*.md` number in
   this worktree is 270, but this packet has no live Jira read authorized
   within its scope (Jira reads outside a dedicated credentialed check were
   not attempted, and the packet forbids any Jira write regardless). Rather
   than guess a CBD number that Jira's live issue numbering might already
   occupy, this document uses the non-CBD name
   `docs/accessibility-conformance-standard.md`, exactly as the packet's
   fallback instructs. If the Manager or Product Owner later assigns this
   document a CBD ticket and number, renaming it and updating
   `config/confluence-publication.json` is a small follow-on change.
2. **Assumption — "the two known contrast exceptions" is two exception
   classes, not two pairing rows.** `contrast-record.md` marks six pairings
   `exempt`, not two. `UI-BUILD-PLAN.md` §5 (OQ-UI-17) and §5.1 both say "the
   two known contrast exceptions" without naming rows. Grouped by the WCAG
   success criterion that exempts them, the six rows fall into exactly two
   classes (§3.1 WCAG 1.4.3 inactive-component text, two rows; §3.2 WCAG
   1.4.11 non-identifying boundaries, five rows) — two classes, six rows.
   This document reproduces every exempt row's rationale verbatim under its
   class rather than picking two rows and omitting four, on the reasoning
   that partial reproduction of a verbatim record would misstate what the
   record contains.
3. **No code change made.** This packet is documentation-only. If any of the
   six exempt pairings' measured ratios or rationale ever need to change,
   that is a `check:tokens` / `contrast-pairings.json` change in a separate,
   focused change — not this document, and not this packet. Nothing in this
   packet's evidence suggests re-pinning is currently needed; §3 restates the
   current record as of baseline `fd90e7f`.
4. **`OI-102-007` (CBD-102) and `RF-71-23` (CBD-71) are not closed by this
   document.** Both are open items living in other approved documents this
   packet does not edit (see §5.2). This document supplies the fact each open
   item was waiting on (an approved conformance standard exists now); closing
   either item's row is for the role that owns that document, citing this one.
5. **Confluence.** No Confluence target exists for this document. It is
   registered `unpublished` in `config/confluence-publication.json`, following
   the `docs/cbd-234-invitations-consent-design-proposal.md` precedent in that
   manifest (§7).
