## How to use this register

Add a feature when a useful idea is identified but should not become an undocumented assumption or expand the active task. An entry is not an approved commitment. During product planning, review each entry and decide whether to reject it, retain it for later review, research it, or create linked Jira work.

Snapshot approval confirms this register accurately records the current working
list and its existing dispositions; it does not approve implementation, expand
MVP scope, or resolve any entry's open questions. Later edits remain working
changes until their exact revised snapshot is approved for publication.

Note on the three matching entries: FF-004, FF-006, and FF-007 concern different problems and none resolves another. FF-004 is matching a *projected* bill or income occurrence to an actual transaction. FF-007 is matching a *pending* authorization to its *posted* settlement. FF-006 is the separate question of whether received cash is ever *allocated* to categories at all.

Note on FF-011 against FF-003 and FF-006: these three touch unspent money but ask different questions, and FF-011 reopens neither approved MVP position. FF-011 is about the *horizon a category target is expressed in* — weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly — and how those are normalized into one funding pace. FF-003 is whether an unspent remainder *carries forward* into a later period; the MVP answer is no, fixed by CBD-71 SD-071-028. FF-006 is whether received cash is *allocated* into categories at all; the MVP answer is no, fixed by CBD-71 SD-071-031's target-and-tracking model. FF-011's open question about "accumulated balances" concerns a savings fund that accumulates *by design* as its stated purpose, which is a different thing from an unspent spending-budget remainder surviving a period boundary. Introducing FF-011 would still require FF-003 and FF-006 to be decided on their own merits.
